## Required correction to the post-freeze disposition

The final addendum sentence says the ESI002 replay criterion is met by the supplement. I do not find that established by the frozen artifact: the supplement establishes body-current identity for a default-export symbol, but does not record the actual conflicting CodeMunch fields needed to show a conflict at that AST boundary. Preserve the supplement as primary replay evidence and keep the trial pending until the exact tool receipt (subject/path/revision, returned source/span, `content_verified`, freshness, Git verification, and receipt ID) is recorded and compared with the immutable span and working bytes. A safe negative should include raw command/tool outcome and expected disposition; local synthetic assertions must remain labeled as such.

This is an evidence/provenance correction, not a request to broaden the trial or change extraction-skill prose. The supplement’s body hash versus whole-blob hash is expected because they identify different byte sequences; it is not itself a conflict. The wrapper note is valuable and should remain separate from claims of public API equivalence.

## Information gain, variation, cost, and counterexamples

The extraction work adds concrete source and packaging facts, but variation does not create a generalized instruction gap: active styling was traced through real consumers, and the acceptance checklist already reserves generated-CSS/runtime proof for those layers. The Codemunch case varies boundary kind (default export versus named function/method) and is worth retaining as a trial observation. It does not yet demonstrate that the new guidance reliably resolves an actual conflicting receipt on that boundary. Adding broad guidance now would cost future readers extra policy text without stronger demonstrated decisions. No target skill or method edit is warranted from this packet.
